# Surface brief: index.html (landing page)

Scope: the single landing page plus its two legal companions (privacy.html, terms.html) which inherit its world. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a solo agency owner who got a cold email, checking whether the sender is real and the data is better than cheap lists, deciding alone and fast. Action: request a free 10-record sample for their ZIPs (name, agency, email, ZIPs). Proof: one worked masked record with live evidence, one example per email tier, one rejected record with its reason, the Miami ZIP list, the seller's real name and contact. Constraints: no invented numbers, no testimonials, no competitor names, no volume claims, no "exclusive", tier mix never hidden, one CTA intent with one label everywhere.

Unresolved decisions (owner): brand name and domain, postal address, tier C counting, tier B default, tier mix numbers, wrong-row definition, freshness days, delivery format and turnaround, Skydo flow, removal address, exact ZIP list, real masked records and portrait photo. Working assumptions are used and marked in source with data-decision attributes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the seller's own sales copy, fact-checked in front of the reader. Every claim carries a source mark, the sentence that proves ownership is highlighted the way a checker highlights a verified passage, and the record the seller refused is struck through with a red pencil. It refuses the category default: the blue-CTA SaaS hero followed by three feature cards and a logo strip.

OWN-WORLD: Newsroom copy desk. White ground and near-black ink, highlighter yellow as the committed color carrying every verified passage and the primary action, red pencil reserved for what was killed, graphite for margin notes. Typewritten manuscript face for records and data (Courier Prime), a contemporary grotesk with optical sizing for the page voice (Bricolage Grotesque). Hairline rules, square corners, margin annotations set in mono with check marks, superscript source numbers, no cards; sections separate with rules and white space.

STORY: The visitor understands within seconds that each record names an owner and shows why. They believe it because they can read the evidence sentence, the source it came from, and one record that was refused. They request a sample because it costs nothing and the ask is four fields.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 1440: a 64px rule-bottomed nav (wordmark, three anchors, yellow "Request a free sample"). Below, a 7/5 split. Left, headline at ~64px in two lines, 18-word subtext, primary yellow button and a text link to the worked record. Right, the manuscript record: a typewritten sheet with fields, the evidence sentence under a yellow highlight, a graphite margin note "Confirmed: About page + Sunbiz filing" with a check mark. Mobile 390: nav collapses to wordmark plus CTA; headline, subtext, CTA, then the record sheet full width.

FORM: Newsroom fact-check manuscript, position 7 of 7 on the ordered grounded list (after spreadsheet, disclosure panel, public-record printout, auditor working papers, affidavit, maps listing). Seed key 8323d474, degraded roll (no challengers, no quality-bar boards). Build path: code-led, no image generation detected, not recorded.

Signature interaction: the highlight over the evidence sentence sweeps in once as the record enters view, and the red strike draws once across the refused record; both are the page's single authored motion, honoring reduced motion.

Honest risk: white plus yellow plus black can read as caution tape if the yellow is overused; the yellow is confined to verified passages and the one action.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
