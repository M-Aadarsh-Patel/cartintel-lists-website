How a record is made

Software gathers.

The business, its website, its Florida company and fictitious-name records, the candidate owners and candidate emails. It checks that each mailbox exists.

A person judges.

I read the sources and answer three questions for every record. A no on any of them and the record is not sold.

A tenth is re-checked.

Before delivery, one in ten records in every order is checked again from scratch.

  1. Is this a real med spa, inside the territory?

    Not a dermatology clinic, not a salon that mentions Botox, not a spa in Fort Lauderdale.

  2. Does the evidence show this person owns it?

    A sentence on their own site, or their name on the state filing. A LinkedIn title alone is not enough.

  3. Is this email really theirs?

    Published beside their name, or built from it and verified, and labeled accordingly.

Researched businesses are never contacted to test an email. Verification is a mailbox check, not a message.
